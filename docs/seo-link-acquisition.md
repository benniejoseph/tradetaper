**Why this exists:** as of July 2026 tradetaper.com has **1 dofollow referring domain**
(31 backlinks, 30 of them nofollow, mostly scraper spam from `.top`/`.icu`/`.party`
domains). Competitors sit at 426–788 dofollow referring domains and DA 56–70.

Technical SEO and on-page content are done and clean. Referring domains are now the
only thing gating rankings. **Target: 10–20 genuine dofollow links.** That is enough to
move from DA 5 into low-double-digits, which is where KD 12–25 keywords become winnable.

Everything below is copy-paste ready. Do them in order — the first tier is highest
value per minute spent.

## Tier 1 — Directories (highest value, ~2 hours total)

Search each site for its "submit product" / "add listing" page rather than trusting a
deep URL, as these move. All accept self-submission.

| Site | Link type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **AlternativeTo** | dofollow | Highest value here. List TradeTaper as an alternative to TradeZella, Tradervue, TraderSync, Edgewonk. You already have comparison pages for all four — link them as the source. |

| **StackShare** | dofollow | List your actual stack (NestJS, Next.js, Postgres). Genuine dev interest. |

| **Crunchbase** | nofollow | Do it anyway — it's a trust signal humans check. |

**Do not** use paid "submit to 100 directories" services. That is exactly the spam
pattern already polluting your competitors' profiles and it will not help you.

## Tier 2 — Launch platforms (one-off, high spike)

- **Product Hunt** — biggest single opportunity. Needs: 3–5 screenshots or a short demo
  video, the tagline above, and a first comment explaining *why you built it*. Launch
  Tuesday–Thursday, 00:01 PT. A modest launch still earns a permanent dofollow link plus
  secondary pickup from aggregators that scrape PH.
- **BetaList** — accepts early-stage products, dofollow.
- **Indie Hackers** — post a genuine build story (the MT5 sync problem is a good hook).
  Links from IH profiles and posts.
- **Hacker News (Show HN)** — low hit rate, but the MT5-sync-without-broker-integration
  angle is technically interesting enough to be legitimate. Post it as an engineering
  story, not a product pitch.

## Tier 3 — Communities (ongoing, highest risk of backfiring)

These are where your actual users are, and also where self-promotion gets you banned.
**Rule: answer questions for weeks before you ever link your own product.**

- **r/Forex, r/Daytrading, r/algotrading, r/propfirms** — journaling questions come up
  constantly. Answer them properly with no link. Mention the tool only when someone
  explicitly asks for a recommendation.
- **Forex Factory** — large, old, high-authority forum. Signature links are permitted on
  established accounts.
- **MQL5 community** — directly relevant given the MT5 EA. Publishing a genuinely useful
  free utility or article here reaches exactly your audience.
- **Trading Discords** — no SEO value (nofollow/invisible) but strong for early users.

**What actually works here:** the four blog posts you already have are answers to
questions these communities ask weekly. Link the *article*, not the product.

## Tier 4 — Things only you can do

- **Ask existing users for reviews** on AlternativeTo / SaaSHub. Two or three real
  reviews outperform twenty directory listings for conversion.
- **Write one guest post** for a trading blog or newsletter. One good contextual dofollow
  from a trading-relevant site beats the entire Tier 1 list combined.
- **Get listed on prop-firm resource pages.** Many prop firms maintain "recommended
  tools" pages. You have a prop-firm feature page to point at, and this is a genuinely
  relevant fit rather than a cold ask.

Re-check monthly:

- **Domain trust** — currently **5**. Expect movement only after ~10 real links.
- **Keyword positions** — expect nothing for 8–12 weeks. New pages on a DA-5 domain

  fine.
